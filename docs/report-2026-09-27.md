## Platform Details

### Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

- **Lifecycle hook decision semantics and 64 KiB context truncation** — the `harness-lifecycle-hooks` page was audited in full for the first time. Beyond the four hook points reported on 2026-09-22, the page documents that only a **Lambda** target can change agent-loop behavior (SNS and EventBridge are notification-only and their `hookEvent` "does not confirm that the target received the event"); that each hook `context` object is capped at **64 KiB**, after which the harness silently replaces the largest fields with `{"truncated": true, "preview": "..."}` and — AWS's own words — "**Context truncation does not activate `failureMode`**"; that a `before_tool_call` decision "controls execution, not visibility in the stream"; that an `after_tool_call` deny "**does not remove the completed tool result**"; and that `UpdateHarness` with a `hooks` list **replaces the entire existing configuration**, with an empty list removing every hook. Max 20 hooks per harness; Lambda `failureMode` is selectable `deny` (default) or `allow`. Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/harness-lifecycle-hooks.html

## New Coverage Required

- **[ENDPOINT] [HIGH] Dia Browser "Tools" — enterprise SaaS connector surface in a consumer AI browser**: Dia 1.49.0 turns the browser into a multi-tenant connector host. Tools now read **Outlook mail and calendar, Teams channel/thread/meeting-chat messages, SharePoint documents, Slack (including voice-note transcripts and screenshots)**, and in beta **Zoom recordings/transcripts, Salesforce accounts/contacts/leads/cases/opportunities, Figma files and comments, and Canva designs**. This is corporate mail, meeting audio and CRM data flowing into an AI browser that sits outside every Microsoft, Salesforce and Zoom governance control Zenity already maps — and it is installed by end users, not admins. New entity types to inventory: Dia tool connections, their grant scopes, the per-user OAuth consents they create against M365/Salesforce/Zoom/Slack tenants, and the "Morning Brief" aggregation surface that concatenates them. ENDPOINT must detect Dia installs and enumerate connected tools; AISPM should treat a Dia tool grant as a third-party app consent on the upstream tenant. The release note documents **no authorization model, no admin control and no audit surface**, and an open request channel implies the catalog will keep growing. Source: https://www.diabrowser.com/release-notes/1-49-0-microsoft-tools

- **[AIDR] [MEDIUM] AgentCore harness lifecycle hooks: silent context truncation and non-authoritative hook events**: Extends the hook surface reported 2026-09-22 with mechanics that determine whether hooks can be trusted as a detection or enforcement feed. A hook's `context` is capped at **64 KiB** and the harness silently substitutes truncation markers for the largest fields — including `toolInput` and `toolResult` — and AWS states that "context truncation does not activate `failureMode`", so a gating or logging Lambda receives partial tool arguments while still being required to return a decision: **the largest (and most interesting) tool payloads are exactly the ones that arrive incomplete**. SNS and EventBridge targets cannot deny anything and their `hookEvent` "does not confirm that the target received the event", with no ordering guarantee outside an SNS FIFO topic — so a hook-based audit trail can lose events without surfacing an error. `before_tool_call` "controls execution, not visibility in the stream" (the model-generated tool request is already visible before the decision), and an `after_tool_call` deny **retains** the completed tool result. Finally, `UpdateHarness` replaces the hook list wholesale and an empty list removes all hooks, making silent de-instrumentation a single control-plane call. AIDR must ingest `hookEvent` while flagging truncation markers rather than treating hook payloads as complete; AISPM should alert on hook lists shrinking, on `failureMode: allow`, and on notification-only targets used where the customer believes they have enforcement. Source: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/harness-lifecycle-hooks.html
